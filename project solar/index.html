What We Detect

Our AI model is trained to identify 6 critical solar panel defect categories with high precision

Hotspot

Localized overheating causing energy loss and potential panel damage. Detected via thermal signature patterns.

High Severity

Scratch

Surface abrasions reducing light absorption efficiency. Identified through edge detection algorithms.

Medium Severity

Delamination

High Severity
Accuracy: 94.5%
